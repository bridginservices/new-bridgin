Plenty of local business owners have "boosted a post" on Facebook, seen a few likes, and concluded that ads don't work. The truth is that **boosting** and **advertising** are very different things. Done properly, Facebook and Instagram (Meta) ads are one of the fastest, most affordable ways to reach nearby customers. Here's the beginner's playbook.

## Start with one clear goal

Every campaign should answer a single question: *what do you want people to do?*

- **Bookings or enquiries** → send them to WhatsApp, a form, or a call.
- **Foot traffic** → promote an offer with your location and hours.
- **Awareness** → introduce your brand to a new local audience.

Trying to do all three at once is why most first attempts fizzle. Pick one.

## Target locally — and tightly

The biggest advantage a local business has is **geography**. You're not competing with the whole country; you just need people near you.

- Set a radius around your shop or service area.
- Layer in relevant interests or age ranges where it makes sense.
- Don't over-narrow — give the platform room to find buyers.

## The ad itself: stop the scroll

People scroll fast. Your ad has about one second to earn attention.

- Lead with a **real photo or short video** — not a generic stock image.
- Say the benefit in the first line ("Custom furniture, delivered in 2 weeks").
- End with a clear action: "Message us", "Book now", "Call today".

## Give it a fair test, then read the numbers

Don't judge a campaign after two days. Let it run, then look at what matters:

- **Cost per result** (per lead, message or booking) — the real number.
- Which **image or headline** performed best.
- Which **audience** responded — then put more budget there.

Kill what isn't working, double down on what is. That loop is the entire game.

## A realistic budget

You can start meaningfully with a modest daily budget — the point early on is to **learn what converts**, not to spend big. Once you know which ad and audience produce results at a cost you're happy with, *then* you scale.

## The bottom line

Meta ads reward clarity: one goal, tight local targeting, a scroll-stopping creative, and honest attention to cost per result. Get those right and a small budget can fill your calendar.
