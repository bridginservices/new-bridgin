"We already have an Instagram page — do we really need a website?" It's the most common question local businesses ask us in 2026. The honest answer: **yes, more than ever.** Here's why.

## You own your website. You rent your socials.

Algorithms change. Accounts get suspended. Reach gets throttled. Your social profiles live on someone else's platform, under someone else's rules. Your website is the one digital asset you truly control.

## Customers still Google you

Before buying, people search. When they find a professional website, trust goes up. When they find nothing — or a broken page — they quietly move to a competitor.

## A website works while you sleep

- It answers common questions 24/7.
- It captures leads through forms and click-to-call.
- It lets customers book, browse or buy on their own time.

## Speed and mobile are non-negotiable

In 2026, more than half your visitors arrive on a phone, and they leave if the page is slow.

- Aim for load times under two seconds.
- Design mobile-first, not mobile-as-an-afterthought.
- Keep the path to "contact" or "buy" short and obvious.

## It's your SEO foundation

You can't rank on Google without a site to rank. A well-structured website is the base every other marketing effort — SEO, ads, content — builds on.

## The bottom line

A website isn't a vanity project; it's your most reliable, always-on salesperson. In 2026, not having one isn't saving money — it's quietly costing you customers.
